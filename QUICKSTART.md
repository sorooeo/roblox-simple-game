## 🤖 Using the AI Chat Tool

Once you open `index.html`, you'll see:

### **Left Side - Create Your Project:**
1. **Choose a template** - Click 🕹️ Movement, 🎯 Game, ✨ Animation, or 🎵 Music
2. **Enter project name** - What's your project called?
3. **Select type** - Movement, Game, Animation, Music, or Custom
4. **Write description** - What should it do?
5. **Pick difficulty** - Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
6. **Preview updates** - See your project details in real-time
7. **Click "Generate .sb3 File"** - Downloads your project!

### **Right Side - AI Chat Assistant:**
Just type and chat! The AI helps with:
- 💡 Project ideas
- 🎮 Game design tips
- 🎨 Animation suggestions
- 🎵 Music player ideas
- ❓ Scratch questions
- ✨ Custom project ideas

## 📥 After You Generate Your .sb3 File

### **Upload to Scratch:**
1. Go to https://scratch.mit.edu
2. Create a free account (if you don't have one)
3. Click **"Create"** or **"My Stuff"**
4. Click **"File"** → **"Load from your computer"**
5. Select your downloaded `.sb3` file
6. Boom! 🎉 Your project is now in Scratch!
7. Edit, remix, and share!
